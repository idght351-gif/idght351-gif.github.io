Baekho Shorts Manager

A private command-line tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel 직장인 백호 (@내돈은내손으로) to manage that one channel. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

Uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own finished Shorts with title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.
  • Adds the owner's videos to the owner's playlists and posts one information comment on the owner's own video.
  • Reads the owner's own channel analytics once a week to make a private performance report.
  • Deletes the owner's own videos only after the owner confirms.
